history_edu Our Heritage

More Than Great Food — A Living Local Tradition.

In the heart of Stonewall County at 121 W 6th St, Video Express & Pizza was born out of a love for two timeless pastimes: the magic of movie night and the warmth of a handcrafted pie straight from the stone hearth.

Though times have changed, our promise remains untouched. Every crust is proofed with patience, every sauce simmered with vine-ripened tomatoes, and every pie hand-tossed by people who call Aspermont home. We believe community happens around comfortable tables, where laughter echoes and neighbors stay a little longer.

"We treat every guest who walks through our doors like family seated at our Sunday dinner."
